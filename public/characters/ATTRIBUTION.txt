Quaternius — Cute Animated Monsters (CC0)
https://opengameart.org/content/textured-cute-monster-pack
Converted OBJ+PNG → GLB for Dungeon Stage.

Included: dragon, demon, ogre, cyclops, yeti, beholder (cthulhu),
lich (ghost), skeleton (skull), bat, wolf (deer/stag).

For a more “premium / cinematic” look beyond free stylized packs:
buy/download higher-poly CC or paid models from Sketchfab, or generate
with Meshy/Tripo, then drop .glb files into this folder using the same names.
